Each organization has one **wallet** of Orca credit. Your balance is everything credited minus everything charged. Credit never expires.

## What credit pays for

| Charge | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Model tokens on an Orca credit model (`orca/openrouter/<id>`) | What OpenRouter charges for the call plus [their fee](https://orcapods.ai/pricing), with no Orca markup. See [models and provider keys](/providers#what-orca-credit-costs). |
| Web searches on an Orca credit model | The provider's search fee ($0.01 a search) plus your model's tokens for reading the results, each plus the same fee, so the price depends on the model: for example about $0.011 on `orca/openrouter/openai/gpt-5.6-luna` and about $0.13 on `orca/openrouter/anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6`. The tokens are charged on the search's **Model calls** row, and the search fee on its **Web searches** row. A search is charged what the provider reported, whatever the outcome. A search on your own provider key is billed by that provider. See [what a search costs](/providers#what-a-search-costs). |
| Machine time past your plan's included hours | Your plan's hourly rate. See [plans](/plans#machine-time). |

Tokens on your own provider key (`<provider>/<id>`) are billed by that provider. Orca charges nothing for them.

## Where credit comes from

- **Signup credit.** A new account gets the signup credit on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) once the person who created the organization adds a card, which is never charged; see [the signup credit needs a card](#the-signup-credit-needs-a-card). It is given once per person: a second organization you create gets none. It is free credit; see [what free credit pays for](#what-free-credit-pays-for).
- **Credit packs.** An admin buys a pack. Each pack's processing fee is taken from the pack; the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) gives the fee and what a pack adds after it. The packs on sale, with the credit each adds and its fee, are listed on the dashboard's **Wallet** page and by `orca billing wallet`.

Plans do not add credit. A plan's included machine time is not a second balance.

## Buy credit

You need the admin role.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Dashboard">
Open **Wallet**. Under **Credit packs**, choose **Buy** on a pack. Pay in the Polar checkout. You come back to the Wallet, which shows "Credit added to your wallet." once Polar confirms the payment.
</Tab>
<Tab title="CLI">
```bash
orca billing wallet          # balance, plan, and the packs on sale
orca billing buy pack:2000   # opens the checkout for the pack priced 2000 cents
```
</Tab>
<Tab title="API">
```bash
curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$ORCA_ORIGIN/api/billing/checkout" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ORCA_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"offer": "pack:2000"}'
# {"url": "https://...polar...", "id": "..."}: open url to pay.
# GET /api/billing/checkouts/{id} reports the checkout's state until the credit lands.
```
</Tab>
</Tabs>

`ORCA_ORIGIN` is `https://api.orcapods.ai`, without `/v1`. The `offer` is `pack:<cents>` for a pack, or `plan:pro` or `plan:max` for a plan.

## The signup credit needs a card

The signup credit is held until the person who created the organization adds a valid payment card in their own name, in the dashboard's **Wallet**. Orca never charges the card for it: the card only confirms a real person and prevents abuse. Your bank may show a temporary authorization, which is released and never collected. Adding a card starts no plan and no purchase. See [add a card for free credit](/dashboard/wallet-and-usage#add-a-card-for-free-credit) for the steps.

- **One signup credit per card, ever.** A card that already unlocked a signup credit, for any organization, unlocks none again. A card that belongs to a banned or suspended user never unlocks one.
- **A card, typed in.** Wallet payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link) cannot unlock it. If your bank declines the card, nothing is saved or charged and you can add another card. After too many attempts in one organization, ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/btTYzdzvqq).
- **Only in the dashboard,** by the organization's creator, signed in. Other members, API keys and the CLI cannot add the card.
- **Skipping it is fine.** Without a card your organization works as before on your own provider keys and on credit you buy. It just has no free credit.
- **Organizations that already had the signup credit:** what is left of it is locked until the creator adds a card. It stays in your balance and keeps its expiry date, but pays for nothing until then. Adding a card runs the same checks and unlocks it.
- **When Orca does not give it,** the Wallet says "Free credit isn't available for this organization". Buy credit to start, or ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/btTYzdzvqq).

Credit Orca grants your organization, such as porting help, needs no card.

## What free credit pays for

Free credit is the signup credit and credit Orca grants. Credit you buy is paid credit.

Free credit pays only for Orca credit models priced at or under a **price ceiling** on input and output tokens. The [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) gives the ceiling. A model is judged on its highest listed price, including its long-prompt tiers: a model whose base price is under the ceiling but whose long-prompt price is over it needs paid credit.

- **Models above the ceiling,** and models with no fixed price (OpenRouter's auto router and other routers), need credit you buy. A card on file does not change this.
- **Web search follows the session's model:** free credit pays for a search when it pays for the model, and the search counts toward the [daily limit](#daily-limit).
- **It is decided when a turn starts,** with the model the turn runs; a price change on OpenRouter applies from the next turn. Subagents follow their lead.
- **Free credit pays first** on a model it covers. On a model above the ceiling, only paid credit pays, and your free credit is left as it is.
- **Credit you buy** pays for any model and is never limited.
- **Machine time** is not affected by any of this.

To see which models free credit covers: the dashboard's model picker marks the others **Paid credit only**, `orca models` marks them `paid credit only`, and `GET /api/models` returns `free_credit: true` or `false` for each Orca credit model. See [models and provider keys](/providers#which-models-free-credit-pays-for).

## Daily limit

Free credit pays for up to a **daily limit** per organization per UTC day. The [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) gives the limit.

- **It counts what calls really cost,** including costs charged later, which count on the day of their call.
- **A call admitted under the limit finishes** and is charged in full, so a day can end a little over the limit.
- **Once the limit is reached,** the next call is paid from credit you bought. With no paid credit to pay for it, it is refused with `free_credit_daily_limit`.
- **It resets at 00:00 UTC.**
- **One model call on free credit runs at a time** per organization. Subagents on free credit wait their turn. Calls on credit you buy never wait for it.
- **Credit you buy is never limited,** and machine time does not count toward the limit.

The dashboard's **Wallet** shows today's free credit spend against the limit, and so does `orca billing wallet`.

## When credit runs low

Orca pauses **paid work** when your balance is below your plan's **minimum balance**, on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) and in the wallet as `min_balance_micro_usd`. Paid work is model calls and web searches on Orca credit, and managed sandboxes, including their included machine time.

- **A new message** that needs paid work is refused with HTTP 429 `insufficient_quota`: "Out of Orca credit: add credit, then continue the session".
- **A running turn** that reaches the minimum ends `failed` with `usage_limit_exceeded` and `param: "orca_credit"`. The conversation is saved. A managed sandbox is paused with its files kept.
- **Turns on your own provider key** in a session without a sandbox keep working.

Nothing switches who pays on its own. To continue, add credit and send the message again (the dashboard's **Continue** button sends "Continue"), or [switch that session to your own key](/providers#switch-who-pays-for-one-session).

The dashboard shows this as **Out of Orca credit** on the turn, with **Add credit**, **Switch who pays**, and **Continue**. `GET /api/billing/wallet` returns `paid_work_paused: true` while paid work is paused.

Free credit has refusals of its own. They come only when credit you bought cannot pay either, and each has a `param` that says why: the signup credit is locked until a card is added (`free_credit_locked`), the model needs paid credit (`paid_credit_required`), today's free credit is used (`free_credit_daily_limit`), or free credit is paused for the organization (`free_credit_paused`). On a model free credit does not pay for, only credit you bought counts toward the minimum balance, and locked signup credit never counts. When paid work for an organization is suspended, a new message is refused with HTTP 403 `organization_suspended`. See [free credit errors](/reference/errors#free-credit-errors) for what to do about each.

## Read the wallet and your usage

| Route | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/billing/wallet` | Balance, total credited and charged, the minimum balance, `paid_work_paused`, your plan and period, included and used machine time, and every field of `GET /api/public/plans` |
| `GET /api/public/plans` | Every plan fact, with no key needed: each plan's price, included machine time, extra machine time rate, sessions at once and minimum balance; the signup credit; OpenRouter's fee (`openrouter_fee_bps`); the processing fee and the packs on sale; the machine; and `idle_seconds`, how long a sandbox idles before it is paused. The pricing page is built from it. |
| `GET /api/usage` | Totals per meter for a period (30 days by default, or `start` and `end` in Unix seconds), a `series` over time (below), and totals grouped by `model`, `provider`, `credential`, `session`, or `agent` with `group_by`. With `group_by`, `group_costs` gives each key's cost across every meter; they add up to the period's `cost_micro_usd`. `session=<id>` narrows it to one session. |
| `GET /api/usage/events` | The newest usage rows, each with its cost. Filter with `meter`, or `charged=true` for only the rows that cost something; page with `limit` and `after`. A row at cost 0 whose money is on another row of the same operation has `charged_on`: `call` when its call's row carries the money (a token row), `search` when the search's row does (the search's tool call, or its call when the whole cost is on the search). Any other row has none, and its own cost is what it was charged. |

For free credit, `GET /api/billing/wallet` also returns:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `signup_credit` | `status`: `needs_card` (add a card to unlock it), `granted`, `withheld` (Orca did not give it to this organization), or `not_eligible` (the person already had one). `amount_micro_usd`; `locked_micro_usd`, the signup credit left that is locked until a card is added (0 otherwise); and `card`, `{funding, country}` of the card that unlocked it, or `null`. Nothing else about the card is ever returned. |
| `free_credit_today_micro_usd` | Free credit spent today (UTC), with costs charged later counted on their call's day |
| `free_credit_daily_micro_usd` | The [daily limit](#daily-limit), or `null` when there is none |
| `free_credit_paused` | `true` while free credit is paused for the organization |
| `suspended` | `true` while paid work for the organization is suspended |

`GET /api/public/plans`, and so the wallet, has a `free_credit` object: `max_input_micro_usd_per_million` and `max_output_micro_usd_per_million` (the price ceiling), `daily_micro_usd` (the daily limit), `in_flight` (how many free credit calls run at once), and `requires_card`, each `null` when off.

Money is in micro-USD (millionths of a dollar). Every member can read the wallet and usage.

`series` is the period's usage over time: `{"interval": "hour" | "day", "points": [{"start", "meter", "quantity", "cost_micro_usd"}]}`. The server picks the interval from the period: `hour` for up to 2 days, `day` for up to 366 days, and `series` is `null` for a longer period. Each point's `start` is its bucket's start in Unix seconds, a UTC hour or a UTC day; a row is in the bucket that holds its `window_start`. Every bucket of the period is there for every meter, at zero when nothing was used, so each meter's points add up to its totals. The first and last buckets are cut by the period.

`daily`, the older per-day series (only the days with usage), is deprecated and is removed on or after 2027-01-11. Read `series` instead. `orca usage` from CLI 1.0.x reads `daily` and fails once it is removed; `orca update` fixes it.

The `web_searches` meter counts the searches the provider ran (unit `searches`), not tool calls: one tool call can run more than one search. Usage recorded before this change counted one per tool call. See [what is counted](/providers#what-is-counted).

A call's money is on its `model_calls` row and its token rows are at cost 0, so read a model's, a session's or an agent's cost from `group_costs`, not from the `model_tokens` totals. The charged rows, the period's `cost_micro_usd`, and the wallet's `charged_micro_usd` over the same rows all agree.

```bash
curl --fail-with-body "$ORCA_ORIGIN/api/usage?group_by=model" -H "Authorization: Bearer $ORCA_API_KEY"
```

In the dashboard, **Usage** shows the same figures by day, broken down by model, session or agent, and as a list of events. From the CLI, `orca usage --group-by <field>` and `orca usage events --charged`.
