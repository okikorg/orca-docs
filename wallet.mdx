Each organization has one **wallet** of Orca credit. Your balance is everything credited minus everything charged. Credit you buy never expires; credit Orca grants does, on a date set when it is granted. See [granted credit and paid credit](#granted-credit-and-paid-credit).

## What credit pays for

| Charge | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Model tokens on an Orca credit model (`orca/openrouter/<id>`) | What OpenRouter charges for the call plus [their fee](https://orcapods.ai/pricing), with no Orca markup. See [models and provider keys](/providers#what-orca-credit-costs). |
| Web searches on an Orca credit model | The provider's search fee ($0.01 a search) plus your model's tokens for reading the results, each plus the same fee, so the price depends on the model: for example about $0.011 on `orca/openrouter/openai/gpt-5.6-luna` and about $0.13 on `orca/openrouter/anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6`. The tokens are charged on the search's **Model calls** row, and the search fee on its **Web searches** row. A search is charged what the provider reported, whatever the outcome. A search on your own provider key is billed by that provider. See [what a search costs](/providers#what-a-search-costs). |
| Machine time past your plan's included hours | Your plan's hourly rate. See [plans](/plans#machine-time). |

Tokens on your own provider key (`<provider>/<id>`) are billed by that provider. Orca charges nothing for them.

The [free model](/plans#the-free-model) on Free costs nothing and needs no credit. On Free, credit pays only for Orca credit models within the [Free price ceiling](/plans#the-free-price-ceiling), their web searches and the free model's, and machine time past the included time. Models above the ceiling run on Orca credit on Pro and Max only.

## Where credit comes from

- **Credit packs.** Paid credit: it never expires. An admin buys a pack. Each pack's processing fee is taken from the pack; the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) gives the fee and what a pack adds after it. The packs on sale, with the credit each adds and its fee, are listed on the dashboard's **Wallet** page and by `orca billing wallet`.

On Free: "Need more to try Orca? Write to us on Discord (https://discord.gg/btTYzdzvqq)." Whether Orca grants credit, how much and for how long is at its discretion, under section 6 of the [Terms of Service](https://orcapods.ai/terms).

No account starts with credit, and plans do not add credit. A plan's included machine time is not a second balance. Signup credit that earlier accounts were given pays under the same rules, including the Free price ceiling.

## Granted credit and paid credit

Your balance has two parts:

- **Paid credit** is credit you bought. It never expires.
- **Granted credit** is credit Orca added at no charge, at its discretion under section 6 of the [Terms of Service](https://orcapods.ai/terms). Each grant has an expiry date, set when it is granted and never moved. The date comes from the grant's reason, by default (`grant_expiry_months` in `GET /api/public/plans`), or is set for that grant. You can use a grant until the end of the day before its `expires_at` (UTC).

How they are spent:

- **Granted credit is used first**, the grant that expires soonest first, so your paid credit is kept. A [late charge](#calls-charged-later) follows the same order at the moment it is written.
- **At expiry, whatever is left of a grant is removed.** It shows in the wallet as `expired_micro_usd`. Paid credit is never touched.
- **An email goes to your organization's admins 15 days before** a grant expires, once per grant, with the amount left. None is sent when nothing is left.

Granted credit pays for the same things as paid credit, on Free within the [Free price ceiling](/plans#the-free-price-ceiling).

The wallet shows each grant with the amount left and its date. In the dashboard, **Wallet** shows the **Granted credit** and **Paid credit** figures and lists each grant. `orca billing wallet` prints one line per grant and one for paid credit:

```text
granted credit  $12.40 left of $20.00, expires 4 Dec 2026
paid credit     $18.20 (never expires)
```

`GET /api/billing/wallet` returns:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `granted_credit_micro_usd` | Granted credit left now |
| `paid_credit_micro_usd` | Paid credit: the balance minus granted credit. It never expires. |
| `granted_credit` | Each grant with something left, soonest expiry first: `[{grant_reason, granted_micro_usd, left_micro_usd, granted_at, expires_at}]`, times in Unix seconds |
| `expired_micro_usd` | Granted credit removed at expiry, in total |

## Buy credit

You need the admin role.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Dashboard">
Open **Wallet**. Under **Credit packs**, choose **Buy** on a pack. Pay in the Polar checkout. You come back to the Wallet, which shows "Credit added to your wallet." once Polar confirms the payment.
</Tab>
<Tab title="CLI">
```bash
orca billing wallet          # balance, plan, and the packs on sale
orca billing buy pack:2000   # opens the checkout for the pack priced 2000 cents
```
</Tab>
<Tab title="API">
```bash
curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$ORCA_ORIGIN/api/billing/checkout" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ORCA_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"offer": "pack:2000"}'
# {"url": "https://...polar...", "id": "..."}: open url to pay.
# GET /api/billing/checkouts/{id} reports the checkout's state until the credit lands.
```
</Tab>
</Tabs>

`ORCA_ORIGIN` is `https://api.orcapods.ai`, without `/v1`. The `offer` is `pack:<cents>` for a pack, or `plan:pro` or `plan:max` for a plan.

## When credit runs low

Orca pauses **paid work** when your balance is below your plan's **minimum balance**, on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) and in the wallet as `min_balance_micro_usd`. Paid work is model calls and web searches on Orca credit, and managed sandboxes, including their included machine time on Pro and Max. On Free, the free model and the included machine time are not paid work: they run at any balance.

- **A new message** that needs paid work is refused with HTTP 429 `insufficient_quota`: "Out of Orca credit: add credit, then continue the session".
- **A new sandbox**, or waking one that is asleep, is refused the same way, and so is a file or skill upload: "Out of Orca credit: add credit to upload files".
- **A running turn** that reaches the minimum ends `failed` with `usage_limit_exceeded` and `param: "orca_credit"`. The conversation is saved. A managed sandbox goes to sleep with its files kept.
- **Turns on your own provider key** in a session without a sandbox keep working.
- **On Free**, a message to an agent whose model runs on credit is refused with HTTP 429 `insufficient_quota` and `param: "free_plan"`: "On Free, this agent's model runs on credit." The message offers three ways on: switch the session to the free model, add your own provider key in **Settings**, **Providers**, or add credit or upgrade.

A call on Orca credit whose cost OpenRouter did not report when it ended, such as a call you cancel, a session deleted mid-reply, a stream cut short or a restart on Orca's side, is charged later from OpenRouter's own record of that call, usually within minutes and at most an hour after it, never from an estimate. Its charge is dated by the call and shows in your usage as **Charged later**. Until it arrives, the call still counts among the paid calls your organization can have running at once, so with several calls cancelled in a row a new call may wait a moment for its turn. Because the charge arrives after the call, it can take your balance below the minimum or below zero; paid work is then paused until credit brings the balance back to the minimum, and a top-up pays what is below zero first.

Nothing switches who pays on its own. To continue, add credit and send the message again (the dashboard's **Continue** button sends "Continue"), or [switch that session to your own key](/providers#switch-who-pays-for-one-session).

The dashboard shows this as **Out of Orca credit** on the turn, with **Add credit**, **Switch who pays**, and **Continue**. `GET /api/billing/wallet` returns `paid_work_paused: true` while paid work is paused.

## Read the wallet and your usage

| Route | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/billing/wallet` | Balance, total credited and charged, the minimum balance, `paid_work_paused`, your plan and period, included and used machine time, the fields below, and every field of `GET /api/public/plans` |
| `GET /api/public/plans` | Every plan fact, with no key needed: each plan's price, included machine time, extra machine time rate, sessions at once, minimum balance and storage limit (`stored_bytes`); the Free price ceiling and the [limits per plan](/plans#limits-per-plan); the free model with its share and daily capacity (`free_model`); OpenRouter's fee (`openrouter_fee_bps`); the processing fee and the packs on sale; the machine; and `idle_seconds`, how long a sandbox idles before it goes to sleep. The pricing page is built from it. |
| `GET /api/usage` | Totals per meter for a period (30 days by default, or `start` and `end` in Unix seconds), a `series` over time (below), and totals grouped by `model`, `provider`, `credential`, `session`, or `agent` with `group_by`. With `group_by`, `group_costs` gives each key's cost across every meter; they add up to the period's `cost_micro_usd`. `session=<id>` narrows it to one session. |
| `GET /api/usage/events` | The newest usage rows, each with its cost. Filter with `meter`, or `charged=true` for only the rows that cost something; page with `limit` and `after`. A row at cost 0 whose money is on another row of the same operation has `charged_on`: `call` when its call's row carries the money (a token row), `search` when the search's row does (the search's tool call, or its call when the whole cost is on the search), `late` when the operation was [charged later](#calls-charged-later) and its `late_charges` row does (every row of that call or search, its call or search row included). Any other row has none, and its own cost is what it was charged. Each row also has `charge_for`: on a `late_charges` row, the id of the row it charges; `null` on every other row. |

The wallet's Free plan fields:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `free_model` | On Free, `{calls_today, calls_per_day, available}`: your organization's free-model calls today, its share, and whether the free model can start a turn now. `null` on Pro and Max. |
| `included_hours_withheld` | `true` when Orca has withheld Free's included machine time and the free model from this organization. Credit you add and your own keys still work. See [free use can be withheld](/plans#free-use-can-be-withheld). |
| `network` | `{"off": true, "message": "Network is off on Free sandboxes until your organization has credit. Add credit, or write to us on Discord (https://discord.gg/btTYzdzvqq)."}` while a Free organization's sandboxes have no network, and `{"off": false}` once it has credit, bought or granted. `orca billing wallet` prints the same line. See [machine time on Free](/plans#machine-time-on-free). |
| `credit_request` | On Free, `{message, url}`: "Need more to try Orca? Write to us on Discord (https://discord.gg/btTYzdzvqq)." and the Discord link. |

`signup_credit_micro_usd` in `GET /api/public/plans` is always 0 and will be removed. Do not read it.

Money is in micro-USD (millionths of a dollar). Every member can read the wallet and usage. `balance_micro_usd` can be negative after a [late charge](#calls-charged-later); paid work stays paused until credit brings it back to `min_balance_micro_usd`.

`series` is the period's usage over time: `{"interval": "hour" | "day", "points": [{"start", "meter", "quantity", "cost_micro_usd"}]}`. The server picks the interval from the period: `hour` for up to 2 days, `day` for up to 366 days, and `series` is `null` for a longer period. Each point's `start` is its bucket's start in Unix seconds, a UTC hour or a UTC day; a row is in the bucket that holds its `window_start`. Every bucket of the period is there for every meter, at zero when nothing was used, so each meter's points add up to its totals. The first and last buckets are cut by the period.

`daily`, the older per-day series (only the days with usage), is deprecated and is removed on or after 2027-01-11. Read `series` instead. `orca usage` from CLI 1.0.x reads `daily` and fails once it is removed; `orca update` fixes it.

The `web_searches` meter counts the searches the provider ran (unit `searches`), not tool calls: one tool call can run more than one search. Usage recorded before this change counted one per tool call. See [what is counted](/providers#what-is-counted).

A call's money is on its `model_calls` row (or its `late_charges` row, below) and its token rows are at cost 0, so read a model's, a session's or an agent's cost from `group_costs`, not from the `model_tokens` totals. The charged rows, the period's `cost_micro_usd`, and the wallet's `charged_micro_usd` over the same rows all agree.

```bash
curl --fail-with-body "$ORCA_ORIGIN/api/usage?group_by=model" -H "Authorization: Bearer $ORCA_API_KEY"
```

In the dashboard, **Usage** shows the same figures by day, broken down by model, session or agent, and as a list of events. From the CLI, `orca usage --group-by <field>` and `orca usage events --charged`.

### Calls charged later

The `late_charges` meter (unit `charges`) counts the calls on Orca credit that ended without their cost and were charged afterwards from OpenRouter's record of the call ([when credit runs low](#when-credit-runs-low)). `GET /api/usage` lists it with its quantity and cost like any meter, and `group_costs` includes it, since the row carries the call's session, provider, model and actor.

Each late charge is one row in `GET /api/usage/events`:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | `<attempt>:late`, in the call's attempt |
| `meter`, `quantity` | `late_charges`, 1 |
| `cost_micro_usd`, `provider_cost_micro_usd`, `fee_micro_usd` | The call's cost, OpenRouter's part of it, and their fee. A web search's whole cost is on its late row. A record of 0 writes a row at 0. |
| `generation_id` | The call's OpenRouter generation |
| `session_id`, `provider`, `model`, `actor` | The call's |
| `credential` | `platform` |
| `window_start` | The call's start, so the charge counts on the call's day, in `series` and in every total |
| `recorded_at` | When the charge was written |
| `charge_for` | The id of the row it charges: `<attempt>:call` for a model call, `<attempt>:search` for a web search |

The call's own rows stay at cost 0 with `charged_on: "late"`. A call that left no rows of its own (a restart on Orca's side, for example) has only its late row, and `charge_for` still names the row its call would have had.

```bash
curl --fail-with-body "$ORCA_ORIGIN/api/usage/events?meter=late_charges" -H "Authorization: Bearer $ORCA_API_KEY"
```
