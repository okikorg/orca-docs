## Check your balance

Open **Wallet**. It shows your plan and the current period, and four figures:

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance** | Everything credited minus everything charged. It says "Paid work paused" when the balance is under your plan's minimum. It can be below zero, for example `-$1.50`, after a call [charged later](/wallet#calls-charged-later). |
| **Credited** | Purchases, grants, and signup credit |
| **Charged** | Platform usage, all time |
| **Machine time** | Time used this period, out of what your plan includes |

When the balance is under your plan's minimum balance, the page says "Paid work is paused" and asks you to add credit. When it is below zero, the page adds "Your balance is below zero. Paid work resumes when credit brings it to `<minimum>`.", with your plan's minimum balance as the amount. See [when credit runs low](/wallet#when-credit-runs-low).

## Buy credit

You need the admin role.

1. Open **Wallet**. Under **Credit packs**, each pack shows its price, the credit it adds, and its fee.
2. Choose **Buy** on a pack and pay in the Polar checkout.
3. You come back to the Wallet. It shows "Credit added to your wallet." once Polar confirms the payment.

If the payment fails, the Wallet says "The payment did not go through. Nothing was charged."

## Change your plan

You need the admin role.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Plans**. Each card shows the plan's price, machine time, extra machine time, sessions at once, and what tokens cost.
2. Choose **Compare** on the plan you want.
3. From Free, choose **Continue to checkout** and pay. From a paid plan, choose **Open billing portal** to change or cancel your plan with Polar.

On a paid plan, **Manage plan** on the Wallet opens the same billing portal. See [plans and machine time](/plans).

## See what you spent

Open **Usage** and pick a period: **24h**, **7d**, or **30d**. The header shows what the period cost. The top row totals model tokens, model calls, compute time, storage, tool calls, and web searches, each with its cost when it has one, and **Charged later**, the calls [charged after they ended](/wallet#calls-charged-later), with their count and cost, when the period has any. **Web searches** counts the searches the provider ran, not tool calls: one tool call can run more than one search. **Tool calls** still counts tool calls. Usage recorded before this change counted one search per tool call. See [web search](/providers#what-is-counted). Three tabs break it down:

- **Over time**: one meter's usage as bars, by the hour on **24h** and by the day on **7d** and **30d**. The label beside the meter says which: **Hourly (UTC)** or **Daily (UTC)**. Bars are UTC hours and UTC days, as are billing periods and every other day Orca counts, so in India a daily bar runs from 05:30 to 05:30 local time. Hover a bar to read its bucket and quantity, for example `11 Oct, 14:00: 2.1K`. The first and last bars are cut by the period, so 24h shows 25 hourly bars unless it starts on the hour.
- **Breakdown**: tokens and cost grouped by model, provider, credential, session, or agent. Each row's cost is everything charged under it, so the Cost column adds up to the header.
- **Events**: the latest usage rows, each with its meter, quantity, cost, session, and model. It opens on **Charges**, the rows that cost something; pick **All meters** or one meter to see the rest.

A call on Orca credit is charged on its **Model calls** row, not on its token rows, and a web search's fee on its **Web searches** row. So in the Events list a row whose money is on another row reads where it is instead of a cost:

- A token row reads **On its call**: its money is on its call's row.
- A web search's **Tool calls** row reads **On its search**: the search it started carries the money. So does a search's call row when the whole cost is on the search.
- Every row of a call or search [charged later](/wallet#calls-charged-later) reads **Charged later**: its **Charged later** row carries the money and shows when the charge was written. A **Charged later** row whose call left no rows of its own is shown on its own, with its model and session.

A row with no such note shows its own cost, and `$0.00` means nothing was charged for it.

A session's own cost, tokens, model calls, web searches, and machine time, each with what it cost, are in its details on the session page, with each model's cost when the session used more than one. An agent's page shows its cost across all its sessions. The same figures come from `GET /api/usage` and `orca usage`; see [wallet and credit](/wallet#read-the-wallet-and-your-usage).
