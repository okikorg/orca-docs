This guide takes you from nothing to a pricing agent of your own. Margin Check holds a company's price list, costs, discount policy and current contracts, and answers two questions about any deal: what it does to gross margin, and who has to approve it. You can ask it from your terminal, or from your coding agent while you work on the code that builds quotes, with nothing to attach.

Orca is where it runs. Tokens cost [OpenRouter's price plus their fee](https://orcapods.ai/pricing), with no markup from us, and the harness underneath is built to spend fewer of them. A sandbox starts only when a session needs one (Margin Check doesn't), a model call that hits a hiccup is retried for you, and every session shows exactly what it cost.

## What it knows

- **A made-up company's pricing.** Larkspur Analytics sells dashboards per seat on three plans, Starter, Team and Enterprise, with add-ons, premium support and an onboarding package. Margin Check holds its prices, its cost to serve each item, nine discount rules numbered R1 to R9, and seven current contracts. All of it is sample data, and step 2 shows it in full.
- **All of it in the agent's instructions.** Nothing is stored in files, so every chat has the data, including one your coding agent starts through Orca's MCP server, where nothing can be attached. To use your own pricing, you edit the instructions: see [make it yours](#make-it-yours).
- **Every figure with its working.** Without a sandbox, Margin Check works out each figure step by step in a table, with the formula and the numbers in it, so you can check every one. Given a sandbox with `--sandbox`, it computes them with Python. Language models can slip on arithmetic, so read the working before you rely on a figure, or use `--sandbox` for the ones that matter.
- **Today's date only when you give it.** For a question like "which contracts end in the next 60 days", put today's date in the question. Without a sandbox to read the clock, Margin Check asks for the date rather than guess it.

```python margin_check_agent.py
        "the term and cost over the term, with onboarding and its processing and cost added once; then gross "
        "margin; total discount; commit allowance; effective discount. Round money to the cent and "
        "percentages to the nearest tenth (76.66% is 76.7%), and only at the end.\n"
        "Then \"Rules:\", one short line per rule that applies, by number, each with the level it requires. End "
        "with R8: list every level those lines require, then name the highest, in the order rep, sales "
        "manager, VP Sales, CFO, as the approver.\n"
        "Then \"Counter-offer:\": the largest total discount the rep could approve alone, and the largest a "
        "sales manager could, each with its gross margin. Find each level's in three steps, and write that "
        "working first:\n"
        "a. The rule limit: the smallest total discount allowed by R1 with the commit allowance (10% plus the "
        "allowance for the rep, 20% plus the allowance for a sales manager) and, for a renewal, by R7 (the "
        "current contract's discount plus 5 points). R5 and R6 must pass too: if they cannot at this level, "
        "for example net 60 terms for the rep, say no discount works at this level.\n"
        "b. The gross margin at that discount, in its own working rows like the deal's: revenue over the "
        "term, cost over the term, gross margin. If it is 75% or more, that discount is the counter-offer.\n"
        "c. Only if it is under 75%: the largest discount with a margin of 75% or more is 1 - F, where F = (K "
        "- 0.22 x O) / (0.22 x A - s x S), and over the term: A is the revenue from subscription, add-ons and "
        "premium support at no discount; S is the subscription revenue at no discount; K is hosting, SSO and "
        "audit log costs, and the onboarding cost; O is the onboarding revenue (3,000 or 0); s is 0.04, or "
        "0.09 with premium support. Check A first: the deal's revenue over the term, less any onboarding, "
        "divided by (1 - its total discount), must equal A; if not, find the mistake. Round 1 - F down to one "
        "decimal place, then work out the margin at that discount as in step b: it must come out at 75.0% or "
        "just over. If it does not, your division is wrong: do it again.\n"
        "Then end with two lines, one for the rep and one for a sales manager, each copying its discount and "
        "margin from the working above. Never state a margin without its working.\n"
        "\n"
        "For a question that is not a single deal, such as which contracts end soon, answer briefly with a "
        "short table and the working behind it.\n"
        "\n"
        "Write plain text and keep it short: no headings and no bold; tables as simple pipe tables. Write "
        "ranges as \"1 to 10\", and never use em or en dashes."
    ),
)
print(margin_check.id)
```

```typescript margin_check_agent.ts
    'audit log costs, and the onboarding cost; O is the onboarding revenue (3,000 or 0); s is 0.04, or ' +
    '0.09 with premium support. Check A first: the deal\'s revenue over the term, less any onboarding, ' +
    'divided by (1 - its total discount), must equal A; if not, find the mistake. Round 1 - F down to one ' +
    'decimal place, then work out the margin at that discount as in step b: it must come out at 75.0% or ' +
    'just over. If it does not, your division is wrong: do it again.\n' +
    'Then end with two lines, one for the rep and one for a sales manager, each copying its discount and ' +
    'margin from the working above. Never state a margin without its working.\n' +
    '\n' +
    'For a question that is not a single deal, such as which contracts end soon, answer briefly with a ' +
    'short table and the working behind it.\n' +
    '\n' +
    'Write plain text and keep it short: no headings and no bold; tables as simple pipe tables. Write ' +
    'ranges as "1 to 10", and never use em or en dashes.',
});
console.log(marginCheck.id);
```
</CodeGroup>

The instructions are the whole program: Orca runs each conversation and keeps every message, and the model follows the rules. `reasoning` gives the model room to work through every rule before it writes the verdict. The model name also says who pays: `orca/openrouter/<id>` runs on your Orca credit. See [models and provider keys](/providers).

## 3. Use it

Ask Margin Check about a deal:

```bash
orca chat margin-check "Brightline wants to renew at 100 Team seats for 24 months at 25 percent off. What does that do to margin, and who approves it?"
```

Brightline Logistics is one of the sample contracts: 80 Team seats at 15 percent off for 12 months, with SSO and the audit log. Margin Check keeps whatever the question doesn't change, works out the figures, and checks the deal against every rule. A real run on 2026-10-16 answered:

```text
Allowed, with CFO approval.

Renewal terms kept: SSO, Audit log, net 30, and no free months. Team supports 100 seats because it requires at least 10 seats and has no stated maximum under R5.

| item | working | amount in USD |
|---|---|---:|
| subscription per year | 100 x $45 x 12 x (1 - 25%) | $40,500.00 |
| SSO per year | 100 x $6 x 12 x (1 - 25%) | $5,400.00 |
| Audit log per year | 100 x $4 x 12 x (1 - 25%) | $3,600.00 |
| revenue per year | $40,500.00 + $5,400.00 + $3,600.00 | $49,500.00 |
| hosting per year | 100 x $6 x 12 | $7,200.00 |
| SSO cost per year | 100 x $1 x 12 | $1,200.00 |
| Audit log cost per year | 100 x $1.50 x 12 | $1,800.00 |
| support per year | 4% x $40,500.00 | $1,620.00 |
| processing per year | 3% x $49,500.00 | $1,485.00 |
| cost per year | $7,200.00 + $1,200.00 + $1,800.00 + $1,620.00 + $1,485.00 | $13,305.00 |
| revenue over term | $49,500.00 x 24 / 12 | $99,000.00 |
| cost over term | $13,305.00 x 24 / 12 | $26,610.00 |
| gross margin | ($99,000.00 - $26,610.00) / $99,000.00 | 73.1% |
| total discount | 25% + 0 points for free months | 25.0% |
| commit allowance | 24-month term | 5.0% |
| effective discount | 25.0% - 5.0% | 20.0% |

Rules:
- R1: Effective discount of 20.0% requires sales manager approval.
- R2: The 24-month term gives a 5.0-point commit allowance.
- R3: The 73.1% margin is between 70% and 75%, requiring CFO approval.
- R5: 100 Team seats is within the stated seat limits.
- R6: Net 30 is standard and requires no additional approval.
- R7: Renewal discount of 25.0% is more than 5 points above the current 15.0%, requiring at least VP Sales approval.
- R8: Required levels are sales manager, VP Sales, and CFO. Highest: CFO.

Counter-offer:

Rep:
a. R1 limit: 10% + 5% commit allowance = 15%. R7 limit: current 15% + 5% = 20%. Smallest limit: 15%.
b. At 15% discount:
- Revenue over term: $132,000.00 x (1 - 15%) = $112,200.00
- Cost over term: $20,400.00 + (4% x $108,000.00 x 85%) + (3% x $112,200.00) = $27,438.00
- Gross margin: ($112,200.00 - $27,438.00) / $112,200.00 = 75.5%
c. Since the margin is at least 75%, no further reduction is needed.

Sales manager:
a. R1 limit: 20% + 5% commit allowance = 25%. R7 limit: current 15% + 5% = 20%. Smallest limit: 20%.
b. At 20% discount:
- Revenue over term: $132,000.00 x (1 - 20%) = $105,600.00
- Cost over term: $20,400.00 + (4% x $108,000.00 x 80%) + (3% x $105,600.00) = $27,024.00
- Gross margin: ($105,600.00 - $27,024.00) / $105,600.00 = 74.4%
c. A = ($99,000.00 - $0.00) / (1 - 25%) = $132,000.00. S = $108,000.00. K = $20,400.00. O = $0.00. s = 0.04.
- F = ($20,400.00 - 0.22 x $0.00) / (0.22 x $132,000.00 - 0.04 x $108,000.00) = 82.5%
- Largest discount at 75% margin: 1 - F = 17.4%, rounded down to one decimal place.
- Revenue over term: $132,000.00 x (1 - 17.4%) = $109,032.00
- Cost over term: $20,400.00 + (4% x $108,000.00 x 82.6%) + (3% x $109,032.00) = $27,239.28
- Gross margin: ($109,032.00 - $27,239.28) / $109,032.00 = 75.0%

Rep counter-offer: 15.0% discount, 75.5% margin.
Sales manager counter-offer: 17.4% discount, 75.0% margin.
```

That answer cost less than a cent. It reads in four parts:

- **The verdict**, in the first line. Needing the CFO is not a refusal: a deal is not allowed only when its margin is under 70 percent (R3) or its seats are outside the plan's limits (R5).
- **The working table**, a row for each figure, with its formula and the numbers in it.
- **The rules** that decided it, by number. Here three apply, and R8 makes the highest of them the approver.
- **A counter-offer**: the largest discount the rep could approve alone, and the largest a sales manager could, each with its margin and how it was found.

Ask about the contracts, too. Give today's date with any question that depends on it:

```bash
orca chat margin-check "Which contracts end in the next 60 days? Today is 2026-10-16."
```

```text
| Customer | Contract end |
| --- | --- |
| Brightline Logistics | 2026-10-31 |
```

To have a conversation, run `orca chat margin-check` with no question, then ask follow-ups such as "And at 20 percent off?" in the same session. Ctrl-C leaves the chat and prints `session session_...`; `orca chat margin-check --session session_...` picks it up again.

### Make it yours

Everything Margin Check knows is in its instructions, so putting in your own pricing is an edit. Open it on the [Agents page](https://app.orcapods.ai/agents), choose **Edit agent**, and replace Larkspur's tables and rules with yours. Or edit `margin_check_agent.py`, delete the old agent with `orca agents delete margin-check --yes`, and run the file again.

- Keep the shape: the price list, the cost to serve, how to compute, the numbered rules, and the contracts. Answers cite rules by their numbers.
- Change "How to compute" with your pricing, such as how your add-ons are discounted.
- The counter-offer's formula uses 0.22, which is 1 minus the 75 percent margin floor of R3 minus the 3 percent payment processing. If you change either, change 0.22 to match.
- Anyone who can read the agent can read your pricing.

<CodeGroup>
```python margin_check.py
import os
import sys

from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(api_key=os.environ["ORCA_API_KEY"], base_url="https://api.orcapods.ai/v1")
agents = client.beta.agents

margin_check = next(agent for agent in agents.list() if agent.name == "margin-check")
session = agents.sessions.create(agent_id=margin_check.id, environment={"type": "none"})
try:
    with agents.sessions.stream(session.id, input=sys.argv[1]) as stream:
        for event in stream:
            if event.type == "agent.session.turn.output_text.delta":
                print(event.delta, end="", flush=True)
            elif event.type in ("agent.session.turn.failed", "agent.session.turn.cancelled"):
                print("\nThe turn ended:", event.type)
    print()
finally:
    agents.sessions.delete(session.id)
```
```typescript margin_check.ts
import OpenAI from 'openai';

const client = new OpenAI({ apiKey: process.env.ORCA_API_KEY, baseURL: 'https://api.orcapods.ai/v1' });
const agents = client.beta.agents;

let marginCheck;
for await (const agent of agents.list()) if (agent.name === 'margin-check') { marginCheck = agent; break; }
if (!marginCheck) throw new Error('No agent called margin-check. Copy it first (step 2).');

const session = await agents.sessions.create({ agent_id: marginCheck.id, environment: { type: 'none' } });
try {
  for await (const event of agents.sessions.stream(session.id, { input: process.argv[2] })) {
    if (event.type === 'agent.session.turn.output_text.delta') process.stdout.write(event.delta);
    else if (event.type === 'agent.session.turn.failed' || event.type === 'agent.session.turn.cancelled') {
      console.log('\nThe turn ended:', event.type);
    }
  }
  console.log();
} finally {
  await agents.sessions.delete(session.id);
}
```

## 6. See what it cost, and clean up

Every call is recorded with what it cost, down to the session:

```bash
orca usage --days 1        # today's usage, by the hour
orca billing wallet        # your balance and plan
```

`orca sessions get session_...` shows one session's cost, tokens, and machine time.

- **Model tokens** are paid from your Orca credit, since Margin Check's model starts with `orca/`. Each answer on this page cost less than a cent.
- **Margin Check uses no machine time**, unless you give it a sandbox with `--sandbox`. Sandboxes use [machine time](/plans#machine-time) from your plan and need a small balance of credit, your plan's [minimum balance](/plans#paid-work-needs-a-minimum-balance).

The numbers for each plan are on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing). See [usage and wallet](/wallet#read-the-wallet-and-your-usage) for the rest.

Keep Margin Check if you like it. To remove everything this page made instead, delete Margin Check's sessions, then Margin Check, then the key. Deleting an agent does not delete its sessions.

```bash
orca sessions list --agent margin-check --all | cut -f1 | xargs -I{} orca sessions delete {} --yes
orca agents delete margin-check --yes
orca keys list
```

Then revoke the key named `margin-check` with `orca keys revoke <its id> --yes`, and close the terminal: its `ORCA_API_KEY` still holds the revoked key.
