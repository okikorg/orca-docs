title: "Plans and machine time"
description: "What Free, Pro, and Max include, how machine time is counted, and what happens at a limit."
---

Every organization is on one plan. The plan sets how much sandbox time is included each period and how many sessions can run at once. Model tokens are not part of the plan: they are paid from your [Orca credit or your own provider key](/providers).

Each plan sets:

- **A price.** Free costs nothing; Pro and Max are billed monthly through Polar.
- **Machine time included** in each period.
- **Extra machine time**, past the included time, at an hourly rate. Pro and Max have one; Free has none.
- **Sessions at once.**
- **A minimum balance** for paid work.

The numbers for each plan are on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing), which is built from the numbers Orca charges with. Every new account gets a [signup credit](/wallet#the-signup-credit-needs-a-card) once it adds a card, which is never charged. Free credit pays only for models at or under a price ceiling, up to a daily limit, both on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing); see [what free credit pays for](/wallet#what-free-credit-pays-for). Credit is bought separately; see [wallet and credit](/wallet).

## Change your plan

An admin of the organization changes the plan:

- **In the dashboard**, open **Settings**, then **Plans**, and choose **Compare** on a plan. From Free, **Continue to checkout** opens a Polar checkout. From a paid plan, **Open billing portal** opens Polar's billing portal, where you change or cancel the plan.
- **From the CLI**, `orca billing buy pro` or `orca billing buy max` opens a checkout, and `orca billing manage` opens the billing portal. See [CLI commands](/cli/commands#billing-and-usage).

Members see the plans but cannot change them.

## Machine time

Machine time is the time your sessions' managed sandboxes run. Sessions with `environment: {"type": "none"}` use none.

- **Every sandbox is the same machine,** the size the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) shows.
- **It is counted in whole minutes** while a sandbox is connected, whether the agent is working or waiting. Each minute a sandbox is running counts as one minute.
- **An idle sandbox is paused** after the idle time the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing) shows. Paused time is not counted. The sandbox resumes, with its files, the next time the session needs it. See [execution environments](/guides/environments#idle-sandboxes-are-paused).
- **The period** is your subscription's billing period on Pro and Max, and the calendar month (UTC) on Free. Included time is counted per period and does not carry over.

When the included time is used up:

| Plan | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Pro, Max | Sandboxes keep running. Each extra minute is charged from your Orca credit at the plan's hourly rate. |
| Free | Sandboxes stop. A running turn ends `failed` with `usage_limit_exceeded`: "This plan's included machine hours for this period are used up". Upgrade to keep going. |

The dashboard's **Wallet** page shows the time used in this period next to what the plan includes.

## Sessions at once

**Sessions at once** is how many of your organization's sessions can have a turn running at the same time. Idle sessions do not count, so you can keep as many conversations open as you like.

Orca checks it when a turn starts. If the limit is already reached, the message is refused with HTTP 429 `rate_limit_exceeded`: "Your plan runs N sessions at once", with your plan's number. Nothing is lost: send the message again when another session's turn ends, or upgrade.

## Paid work needs a minimum balance

Even included machine time needs Orca credit in the wallet. When your balance is below your plan's **minimum balance** (on the [pricing page](https://orcapods.ai/pricing), and in the wallet as `min_balance_micro_usd`), Orca pauses paid work: model calls and web searches on Orca credit, and managed sandboxes. A turn on an Orca credit model, or in a session with a managed sandbox, is then refused. Turns on your own provider key in a session without a sandbox keep working, their web searches included. See [wallet and credit](/wallet#when-credit-runs-low).
